# Patent and publication control

The requested destination `BlockChain-BailBonds/Software-Defined-Computer` is PUBLIC. As a result, source and architectural descriptions committed here are broadly disclosed, can be indexed and copied, and cannot reliably be preserved as trade secrets after publication. Copyright and an all-rights-reserved notice do not reverse disclosure.

Potentially patentable subject matter requires a qualified patent attorney or agent to evaluate novelty, non-obviousness, enablement, inventor identity, eligibility and prior art. Filing first is generally preferable to public disclosure, especially if protection outside the United States is intended. Certain jurisdictions permit limited grace periods while others offer little or none. No filing or provisional application is made by this GitHub push.

## Evidence that should be privately preserved

- Complete source archives and timestamps, independently generated SHA-256, signed release tags and source provenance.
- Contemporaneous design logs, revision history, tested prototypes, private lab notes and verified inventor contributions.
- Written work-for-hire/assignment agreements where applicable and third-party license compliance review.
- Communications with patent counsel and filings; do not commit privileged drafts or confidential claims into a public repository.

## Disclosure boundary

Do not add unpublished detection rules, model weights, embedded firmware extraction, private wire protocols, business trade secrets or device secrets here under the mistaken belief that a restrictive LICENSE makes them confidential. Seek counsel for a private invention capture process and timely filings.
