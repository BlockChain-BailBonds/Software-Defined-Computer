## Rights reservation and enforcement

- The repository's license intentionally grants no broad public software reuse rights. See `/LICENSE`; platform Terms of Service and third-party licenses still apply.
- No patent grant is offered. A GitHub date alone does not prove inventorship, novelty, ownership or patent priority.
- Any patentable claims require prior-art research, competent inventor identification, legal evaluation and timely filing.
- Publicly posted technical details cannot continue to be treated as undisclosed trade secrets. Keep undisclosed know-how in a properly access-controlled private system under suitable confidentiality agreements.
- To make future provenance defensible, retain original source ZIPs, their SHA-256 hashes, authorship logs, hardware test records, assignment agreements and verified signed tags in separately access-controlled storage.
