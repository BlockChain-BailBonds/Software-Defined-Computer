# Contribution and rights policy

This repository is source-available, all rights reserved. Public visibility is not an open-source license or a blanket authorization to fork and redistribute outside the permissions GitHub's Terms of Service requires.

Unsolicited copyrighted code, patent disclosures, confidential inventions and third-party material should not be submitted through public issues or pull requests. Prior to accepting any material contribution, the maintainer will require an explicit, independently reviewed written contributor or assignment agreement covering authorship, the necessary reproduction/modification/distribution rights, patent grants where appropriate, and the contributor's warranty of authorization. A GitHub account name or a commit alone does not transfer ownership.

Bug reports may describe reproducible non-confidential failures without including credentials, customer data, unpatched exploitation details or proprietary third-party source. Do not assume that submitting a public issue preserves any secrecy or patent filing date.
